A $25 CAD non-refundable deposit via e-transfer secures your appointment slot. This goes directly toward your total service balance.

Located in Northeast Calgary, AB. Exact private studio address and parking details are provided 24 hours prior to appointment.

Please arrive on time. A 15-minute grace period is provided. Cancellations require 48 hours advance notice.

I do not work over foreign acrylics. Please arrive with bare healthy nails or book a soak-off removal.
